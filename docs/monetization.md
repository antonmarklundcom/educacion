# Monetization — feedback on the USD 50/program/year idea, and a model

## 1. Direct feedback on your proposal

> "jag vet inte exakt hur jag ska tjäna pengar men typ 50 usd per utbildning per år hade kunnat vara en ok start"

**The price level is about right. The unit of sale is wrong.** Three concrete problems:

**a) It creates a line-item negotiation with the wrong person.**
A university marketing director signs one contract per year. Per-program pricing invites them to open a spreadsheet and ask "do we really need to pay for Filosofía?" — and they will drop exactly the low-enrolment programs that make our index look complete. We need 100% coverage; the pricing model should never reward partial coverage.

**b) USD 50 ACV cannot pay for the sales motion.**
Selling to Paraguayan institutions means a WhatsApp thread, a meeting in Asunción, a follow-up, and a factura. That is 3–6 hours of human time per close. At USD 50 you are underwater on the first program and only profitable if you land 30+ per account — which is exactly the conversation the per-program unit makes harder. **Minimum viable ACV is ~USD 500 per institution.**

**c) The dangerous version of this model is "pay to be listed".**
If only paying programs appear, the index becomes an ad board: incomplete, useless for students, un-rankable in Google, and the comparador becomes a lie. The whole business dies. **Listing must always be free and exhaustive.** We sell _enhancement and access_, never _presence_.

**The fix:** same money, one signature. Institution-level annual plan, priced in program bands, landing at an effective **USD 20–60 per program per year** — your number, arrived at without any of the above problems.

## 2. The market, sized honestly

| Segment                                   | Count (approx.) | Willingness to pay                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Private universities                      | ~40             | **High** — they compete on enrolment and already buy Facebook/Instagram ads and radio                                  |
| Public universities (UNA, UNE, UNI, UNP…) | ~10             | **Near zero** — no marketing budget, no incentive. They stay free forever, and they drive a large share of our traffic |
| Institutos técnicos superiores / IFD      | ~40–60          | **Medium** — smaller budgets, but high intent and less crowded ad channels                                             |
| Posgrados / MBA / educación ejecutiva     | overlaps above  | **Highest per lead** — a maestría lead is worth 10× a grado lead                                                       |
| Cursos & certificaciones (Phase 4)        | hundreds        | Medium volume, low ACV, self-serve only                                                                                |

**Realistic sellable universe: ~70–100 institutions.** That is a small, finite, reachable list — which is good news for sales (you can name every prospect) and bad news for ceilings (this is not a venture-scale market).

Total offerings in the country are on the order of 6,000–10,000 including posgrados and sedes. ANEAES counts ~2,565 accredited active grade programs alone.

## 3. Recommended model

### Phase 0–1: **charge nothing.** (months 0–6)

Everything listed, everything free, no ads. The only goals are index completeness, Google index age, and being _the_ reference during the accreditation story. Trying to monetize before there is traffic burns the relationships you need later.

### Phase 3: institution plans

| Plan                                | Price (USD/year)   | What the institution gets                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gratis**                          | 0                  | Listed from public data. Basic profile. No lead delivery (leads visible as count only), no editing.                                                                   |
| **Verificado** — hasta 25 programas | **490**            | Claimed profile, edit own data, logo + photos + video, full lead delivery (email + WhatsApp), "Perfil verificado" badge, basic stats.                                 |
| **Verificado** — 26–75 programas    | **890**            | idem                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Verificado** — 76+ programas      | **1.490**          | idem                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Destacado** (add-on)              | **+1.200 – 3.000** | Priority placement in up to N career hubs (clearly labelled "Destacado"), banner on the relevant `/areas/*` page, featured in the comparador sidebar, monthly report. |

Effective per-program price: **USD 20–60/year.** Same instinct, better packaging.

### Why this works where per-program doesn't

- One decision, one contract, one invoice, one renewal date.
- The band structure means big universities pay more without a negotiation.
- Free tier keeps the index complete, so the product stays honest and rankable.
- "Verificado" sells a _badge students care about_ — in a country where 82% of programs are unaccredited, "perfil verificado por la institución" is real, sellable trust.

### Phase 3+: pay-per-lead (once volume exists)

Once the site produces **300+ leads/month**, introduce lead packs: included quota per plan, then **USD 4–8 per verified lead** (verified = valid Paraguayan mobile, deduplicated, not discarded within 48 h). LATAM education CPL benchmarks sit roughly in the USD 3–12 range; price at the low end initially to prove the channel.

CPL is the model institutions understand fastest ("te mando 100 interesados"), but it cannot come first — you need volume before you can sell it, and a CPL-only model early makes revenue swing wildly with seasonality.

### Adjacent revenue (Phase 4, in priority order)

1. **Posgrados & educación ejecutiva** — highest value per lead, buyers with real budget, least served by existing portals.
2. **Cursos/certificaciones self-serve** — a USD 15/month self-serve tier for small academies. Pure margin, zero sales effort, but only build it after the self-serve panel exists.
3. **Becas listings** — sponsored placement by foundations/banks (Itaú, Banco Familiar-type CSR programs).
4. **Editorial sponsorship** — the accreditation hub will be the most-linked content; sponsor it rather than running display ads.
5. **Data/reporting** — an annual "Estado de la oferta académica en Paraguay" report. Low revenue, high authority and press value. Consider giving it away as the best marketing you have.

**Explicitly not recommended:** display advertising (destroys the UX and CWV for cents), and paid rankings that aren't labelled (destroys the only asset you have).

## 4. Revenue projection

Deliberately conservative. Assumes solo/part-time operation with agent assistance.

**Year 1 (post-launch, one admission cycle):**

|                             | Conservative | Base              | Optimistic         |
| --------------------------- | ------------ | ----------------- | ------------------ |
| Institutions on a paid plan | 8            | 15                | 25                 |
| Average contract value      | 650          | 750               | 900                |
| Subscription revenue        | **5.200**    | **11.250**        | **22.500**         |
| Destacado add-ons           | 0            | 3 × 1.500 = 4.500 | 7 × 1.800 = 12.600 |
| **Total ARR (USD)**         | **~5.200**   | **~15.750**       | **~35.100**        |

**Year 2**, with a full season of index age, the accreditation hub ranking, and CPL live: **USD 40k–90k** is a defensible target.

**Read this honestly:** Year 1 is a side business, not a company. That is fine — the cost base is one Hostinger slot (already paid), a domain (already owned), and a part-time data assistant. But do not build a Year-1 plan that requires Year-2 revenue.

**Costs to plan for:**

- Data assistant (relevamiento + verification): the only real cost. Budget ~USD 250–450/month part-time during Phase 1–2, seasonal after.
- Email sending, OG image generation: negligible.
- Hosting: existing slot.

## 5. Billing operations in Paraguay (do not skip this)

- **Payment reality:** transferencia bancaria dominates B2B. Card-on-file SaaS billing is not the norm for institutional buyers.
- **Phase 3 billing is manual and that is correct:** an admin marks a subscription active with an invoice reference. Do not build Stripe-style billing for 15 customers a year.
- **Facturación:** you need to issue a legal factura with IVA 10%. You already run an invoicing system (FacturaPY) — issue from there and store only the `invoice_ref` in `subscriptions`. Do not rebuild invoicing inside this app.
- **Currency:** quote in USD (stable reference for annual contracts), invoice in guaraníes at the day's rate. Store `price_usd_year` on the plan and the guaraní amount actually invoiced on the subscription.
- **If a payment gateway is ever needed:** Bancard (vPOS) and Pagopar are the local options. Evaluate only when self-serve cursos (Phase 4) exists — not before.
- **Contract timing:** annual, aligned to the admission cycle. Sell Aug–Oct, run Nov–Oct. A January renewal date sells against an empty budget.

## 6. What would make this genuinely bigger

Three plausible expansions, in order of likelihood:

1. **Regional replication.** The same codebase for Bolivia/Uruguay is mostly a data problem, and the schema in `data-model.md` is already country-agnostic (departments/cities/agencies are data, not code). Keep it that way — no hardcoded "ANEAES" in the UI, use the `agency` field.
2. **B2B2C with colegios.** Secondary schools need orientación vocacional material. A free tool for colegio counsellors is a distribution channel to the exact cohort, one year before they search.
3. **The jobs side.** You mentioned comparing "utbildningar och jobb". A carrera→empleo bridge (which programs feed which roles, which employers hire from which institutions) is the most defensible long-term product — and the one place where a real paid data asset could exist. It is also the one with the biggest fabrication risk. See `risks.md` §R-11: **do not ship salary or employability numbers without a citable source.** Build it as "empleos relacionados" (real job postings) before "salario promedio" (invented statistics).
